Automated paper trading — strategies, portfolio and analysis

Simulated orders only. Every fill is priced at the official implied YES ask, charged the official taker fee, written to an append-only local ledger, and settled only when the API reports a result. No order is ever sent to Kalshi and no key is used.

Read this before trusting a number. Paper fills are not fills. The demo book is a test venue with thin depth, so an order this page would take may not have been fillable at that size in a real book. Results here are a way to compare rules, not evidence of profit.

How the simulation works, line by line
Entry price
The official implied YES ask from the order-book response (or the market's yes_ask_dollars when no book is loaded). Never the last trade, because the last trade is not a price you can buy at. Source ↗
Fee
Official taker fee: ceil_to_cent(M × 0.07 × C × P × (1−P)). The fee implementation is asserted against all 21 rows of the published table in the test suite. Fee schedule ↗
Payout
$1.00 per contract on the winning side of a simple binary market; zero on the losing side. A scalar result uses the API's settlement_value_dollars. Source ↗
Universe
Open markets in the five topics only, after the screen. At most six open positions at a time, ten contracts each, so the rules are comparable on a $1,000 paper bankroll.
What is NOT modelled
Queue position, partial fills, slippage beyond the touch price, borrow or margin, and market impact. Real fills would be worse or absent.

The ledger lives in this browser (localStorage key tradeDesk.ledger.v1). Clearing site data erases it. No Kalshi account, key or order is involved.
