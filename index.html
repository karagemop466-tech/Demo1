03 / Scope and honesty

What this site does — and what it refuses to do

What it does

  • Reads live public demo data: markets, events, order books, candlesticks.
  • Ranks markets by named dollar metrics with the formula shown.
  • Charts contract price over time at seconds / minutes / hours / days granularity.
  • Shows contracts and dollar value resting at each price level.
  • Tracks each market's lifecycle and records how it resolved (YES / NO).
  • Reports demo-account balance, positions, fills and realised P&L when a key is connected.

What it will not do

  • Invent, interpolate or carry forward a value it did not receive from the API.
  • Present contract volume as “dollars” without naming the metric used.
  • Publish, store or transmit a Kalshi private key.
  • Place an order without an explicit, signed confirmation from the operator.
  • Claim a result before the exchange reports one.
