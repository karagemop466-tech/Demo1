What the snapshot cannot tell us

  • Whether a trade would fill at the displayed price. That needs the live orderbook at order time.
  • Which strategy works. There are no trades or results yet.
  • Whether a $0.01 or $0.001 price is a real probability or a stale or thin quote. Needs trade history (candlesticks and the trades endpoint, both documented by Kalshi).
  • Fees. Series records show fee_type: quadratic, but Kalshi's fee schedule was not read in this session. Net returns cannot be computed without it.
  • Settlement outcomes. No sampled market has resolved yet, so no yes/no result is recorded.
