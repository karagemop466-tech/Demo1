Suggestions queue

These are proposed improvements—not claims that they are already built or proven to improve trading results. Review each item, compare its official source, and mark a preferred path before future implementation work.

Decision behavior

Approve / defer / reject choices are stored only in this browser's local storage. They do not modify the repository, publish a change, or authorize a trade. A durable review workflow would need an explicitly chosen shared system.
