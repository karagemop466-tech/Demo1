Review process

  1. Read the suggestion and its expected decision-making benefit.
  2. Choose a path: approve, defer, or reject — record it in the Decision column.
  3. If approved, implement on a branch, verify against official Kalshi docs, then merge.
  4. Rejected items stay visible with the reason noted.

Review choices are stored in this browser only (local storage); they are not sent to the site or published and are not implementation approval.
