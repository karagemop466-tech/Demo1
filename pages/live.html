Live events ranked by dollar size

Ranking metric is traded notional (USD) = volume_fp × notional_value_dollars summed over every market in an event, using the field definitions published in the official Market schema. It is a dollar figure derived from official fields — it is not “money at risk”, and it counts all lifetime volume, not only the selected window. The window only selects which markets are in scope (those that printed trades inside it).
