Trades & strategies

This page turns fills into a reviewable ledger: it normalises them against the official fill schema (count_fp, yes_price_dollars, no_price_dollars, fee_cost, outcome_side, book_side, is_taker, created_time), tags each trade with an auditable strategy rule, and joins the result to the resolution log so realised P&L appears only once the market actually resolves. Before that, the row shows “open”.

1 · Where the fills come from

Account data is authenticated. The key never lives in this static site: tools/kalshi-signer.mjs runs on your machine, signs timestamp + METHOD + path (RSA-PSS/SHA-256 or Ed25519) and adds the CORS headers this page needs. Without the bridge, the account columns stay blank — see Flagged for review.

2 · Import or record fills

Imported rows and locally-recorded paper entries live in this browser only (localStorage.kd.journal) and are marked with their source: the endpoint URL for API fills, “paper entry” for manual ones.

How results feed back into strategy review

  • The resolution tracker records result = yes/no per market with the API URL that proved it.
  • On load, this page joins that log to your fills, so realised P&L is computed from official settlement facts, not from a price guess.
  • Unsettled rows stay “open”; open positions are counted separately from settled ones so the two never mix in a ratio.

What still has to be decided

  • Which fills to trust as the book of record (API fills vs local journal) after a reload.
  • Whether fees use fee_cost as returned or the published schedule — see Kalshi fee schedule.
  • Risk limits per market / per topic, and who is allowed to press “place order”. Listed on the suggestions board.
