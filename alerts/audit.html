3. QLM high-ROI model (formula and weights published on the desk)

ComponentWeightStatusNotes
Move size25%Official fieldsAdjacent close delta absolute value.
Fee efficiency15%Official fieldsKalshi taker fee schedule applied to official price.
Resting depth20%Official fieldsOrder-book levels at/near the move.
Measured 24-hour traded notional15%Official fieldsPublic trades in the window.
Time to close10%Official fieldsMarket expiry/close from the market record.
Recency15%Computed1 / (1 + age_hours / 6); missing official values dropped with explicit reasons.

Formula: 100 × SUM(weight × normalised component) / SUM(weight of available components). Missing official values are dropped and the denominator re-normalised. This is a disclosed ranking heuristic, not a probability model; it asserts no win probability.

Official fee reference: Kalshi Fee Schedule (July 7, 2026) — taker fee roundup(M × 0.07 × C × P × (1 − P)); maker fee roundup(M × 0.0175 × C × P × (1 − P)); no settlement or membership fee.
