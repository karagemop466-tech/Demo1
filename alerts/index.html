How each number on this page is produced

Shown valueFormula / fieldOfficial source
Move (from → to, Δ)Adjacent official candlesticks; the close that changed by at least the threshold. Null closes are skipped, never filled. ↗ Get Market Candlesticks
Candle close usedprice.close_dollars (traded) or the mid of yes_bid/yes_ask (book). Each line states which basis it used. ↗ Get Market Candlesticks
Resting depthOrder-book level [price_dollars, count_fp] within $0.02 of the post-move price. Kalshi publishes bids only; a YES ask is 1 − best NO bid. ↗ Orderbook responses
24 h traded notionalΣ(yes_price_dollars × count_fp) over public trades in the window. Kalshi publishes no “dollar volume” field, so this is a computed measure. ↗ Get Trades
Fee in the scorefees = round up(M × 0.07 × C × P × (1−P)) for a 10-contract clip, twice (in and out). M is the published per-series multiplier, otherwise 1. ↗ Fee schedule (PDF)
Time to closeclose_time from the market object, against the snapshot time. ↗ Get Markets
Resolved YES / NOstatus and result on the market object. result can be yes, no or scalar; a YES holder is paid $1 per contract, a NO holder $1 per contract on the other side. ↗ Market lifecycle · ↗ Settlement

  • No credentials, no orders. This page uses only public market data. Authenticated balance, position and fill figures need a demo API key, which is deliberately not part of this static site. Flagged for review ↗
  • No cause is asserted. The news-search link on each line is a starting point for your own check, not evidence of what moved a price.
