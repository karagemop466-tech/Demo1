What the trades actually did

Trades are grouped by topic, strategy, side and price band, and scored against the resolution the exchange reported. A trade only counts as won or lost when its market has settled; everything still open is reported separately as unresolved.

How a trade is scored

Cost = contracts × fill price (the YES price for a YES fill, the NO price for a NO fill). Payout = $1 per contract when the reported result matches the traded side, otherwise $0. Realised P&L = payout − cost − fees.
