Contract price over time

The x-axis is time and the y-axis is the current contract price in dollars. Choose the granularity and how much history to show; the chart only draws points the API actually returned.

About the x-axis options

The official candlestick endpoint accepts period_interval of 1, 60 or 1440 minutes only — one minute, one hour, one day. There is no sub-minute server-side candle.

Seconds is therefore produced by sampling the live public quote in your browser at the chosen cadence and plotting those client-side ticks. It is real API data, but it is sampled by this page rather than served as an official candle series.
