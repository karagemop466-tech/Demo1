Official pages read for this page

  • Test in the demo environment — mock funds; demo prices may not reflect real markets.
  • API environments and endpoints — the demo REST and WebSocket bases used here.
  • Quick start: market data — market data needs no authentication.
  • GET /markets, GET /markets/{ticker}, GET /markets/trades, GET /markets/{ticker}/orderbook, GET …/candlesticks.
  • Market lifecycle — result is set at determined, may be challenged at disputed, and payout is finalized.
  • Market settlement — a winning contract pays $1.
  • Orderbook responses — the book returns yes bids and no bids; a no bid at x is a yes ask at 1 − x.
  • Exchange sharding — exchange_index routing and the market-level exchange_index field.
  • Quick start: WebSockets — the WS handshake requires a signed key, even for public channels.
