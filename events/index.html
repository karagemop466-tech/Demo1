Market data / lifecycle

Events, tracked until they resolve

Each market follows a documented lifecycle — initialized, active, closed, determined, finalized — and the result is set to yes, no or scalar. This page follows the events in scope and records how each one ended.

Lifecycle reference
initialized
created, not yet open
active
open for trading
inactive
paused by the exchange
closed
past close_time, awaiting determination
determined
result known, settlement timer running
finalized
settled, positions paid out
