Trade categories & strategy catalogue

Strategies, grouped by how the trade makes money

Each category lists the observable inputs the dashboard actually collects, so a strategy can be judged against data rather than intuition. Performance for any of them requires the demo-account fills that are blocked on credentials (see Portfolio).

Spread screen on the most-traded tracked markets

Computed spread = 1.00 − best YES bid − best NO bid, in cents. A positive number means the two sides cannot be bought for less than $1.00; a negative number means a crossed/arbitrage-like book exists at the quoted quotes. This uses API quote fields only and ignores size — check the ladder on Liquidity before believing any of it.
