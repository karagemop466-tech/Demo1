Questions, information and answers

Read this before trusting any number. These are the questions that must be answered before a trading or design decision can be made fairly from what we have. For each: what is known from an official source, what is genuinely missing, and the specific input that would resolve it.
#QuestionWhat we know (official)What is missingWhat would resolve itSource
Q1Is there a Kalshi demo API key available for this project, and may a server-side worker use it?No key exists in the repository or the build environment (verified by inspection).Every portfolio, fills and settlement figure is blank because of this — not because the API failed.A read-only demo key issued from demo.kalshi.co. Until then portfolio analysis cannot be verified.official ↗
Q2Should the dashboard be allowed to place real orders on the demo exchange?Order placement requires a signed request to POST /portfolio/events/orders.No order has been placed and no order form is live.Explicit go/no-go plus risk limits (max order size, daily loss cap, permitted topics, kill switch).official ↗
Q3What exactly does “highest dollar amount live events” mean?Three defensible readings: dollars traded, dollars of resting depth, open interest × price.The dashboard currently shows a computed 24 h traded notional and labels it as such.A definition. Until chosen, ranking is disclosed as one interpretation, not the only one.official ↗
Q4Is the demo exchange's liquidity representative of production?Demo and production are separate environments with separate credentials and separate books.Conclusion: demo depth cannot be used to estimate real fill sizes.Confirm that strategy calibration on demo data is acceptable, or provide a production read-only key.official ↗
Q5Which series belong to each of the five topics?The API exposes discovery categories and tags, but no project-specific topic taxonomy.Keyword/category rules are published per topic page with the match reason for every series.Approve or edit the series allowlist per topic.official ↗
Q6How should fees and net edge be computed?Each series reports fee_type and fee_multiplier; fees are quadratic and price-dependent.Gross spreads are shown and explicitly labelled as pre-fee.Verification of the fee formula against the official fee schedule before any net figure is published.official ↗
Q7How fresh must prices be?REST candlesticks support only 1/60/1440-minute buckets and can trail trades by seconds; streaming needs a signed WebSocket session.Charts show bucketed official history, not tick data.A refresh target (e.g. sub-minute) decides whether streaming is required.official ↗
Q8What is the minimum sample before a strategy performance claim is shown?Nothing on the site currently claims strategy performance.Win rates and averages are withheld until closed trades exist.A threshold (e.g. ≥ 30 closed trades) below which the dashboard reports “insufficient sample”.official ↗
Q9Should resolved markets outside the five topics be tracked?Collection is limited to matched series by design.Resolved tables include only gas, elections, culture, crypto, golf.Confirm the scope limit is intended.official ↗
Q10Who reviews the flagged irregularities, and how often?Flagged items are published openly on the site.Irregularities do not block publication; they are listed with sources.A review cadence and an owner.flagged.html

Information we do not have (and did not invent)

  • Demo-account cash, positions, fills, fees and realised P&L — no key.
  • Fee-adjusted edge for any market — fee formula not yet verified per series.
  • Production liquidity, order-book depth and slippage — demo books are separate.
  • Seconds-level price history — not offered by REST candlesticks.
  • Any forward-looking forecast — out of scope; the dashboard reports market prices, not our predictions.
