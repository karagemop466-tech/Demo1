Resolution tracking. Markets are collected twice: with status=settled (which the official Market Lifecycle page maps to finalized) and with status=closed (past the close time but not yet finalized, so still tracking). The outcome is the API's own result field (documented values: yes / no / scalar); the event's settlement_sources give the official outside source used to determine the market. A market with an empty result is shown as “no result field” rather than guessed.
