Resolved outcomes (YES / NO)

Resolution tracking. Markets are collected with status=settled, which the official Market Lifecycle page maps to the finalized state. The outcome is the API's own result field (documented values: yes / no / scalar); the event's settlement_sources give the official outside source used to determine the market. A market with an empty result is shown as “no result field” rather than guessed.
