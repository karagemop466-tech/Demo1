How to read this table. Prices are in cents of a dollar (100¢ = $1.00 payout). “Bid / ask” are the API's own quote fields; a quote with zero size behind it is not a tradeable level (see the market page). “Notional 24h” is computed: Σ(yes_price_dollars × count_fp) over public trades in the last 24 h from Get Trades, with the number of trades counted shown — it is a measure of dollars traded, not dollars of resting liquidity. Rows are sorted by that computed notional; ties fall back to the API's own volume.
