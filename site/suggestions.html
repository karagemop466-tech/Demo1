This page is a review queue, not a changelog. Nothing here is implemented automatically. Each item states the change, why it improves decisions, what is needed, and whether it is blocked on a decision from you. Pick the ones worth doing and they can be sequenced.

How to choose between them

  1. Unblock measurement first. Items 1 and 2 are the only ones that turn this dashboard from a market viewer into a performance tracker. Everything else is theory until fills exist.
  2. Fix definitions before numbers. Items 6 and 7 change what the headline figures mean; do them before anyone makes a trading decision from the dashboard.
  3. Then widen the data. Streaming and historical snapshots (3 and 5) improve fidelity once the definitions are stable.
