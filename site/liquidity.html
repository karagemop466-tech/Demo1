Liquidity, depth and price movement

What the order book really is. Kalshi returns bids only. A YES bid at price X equals a NO ask at 1.00 − X, so the ask side is derived, never quoted from a separate field (Orderbook Responses). Every level below is a live resting bid with the contract count available at that price. Prices are dollars; 0.42 = 42¢.
