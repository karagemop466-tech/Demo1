Price swing alerts (≥ $0.10)

A simplified scrolling alert feed of contract-price swings of $0.10 or more in either direction, detected between consecutive official candle closes on the Kalshi demo exchange. Two scrolling chats: All swings shows every detected move; High-ROI filter shows a selective subset using a disclosed magnitude heuristic (not a predictive model, no real money). Markets with no qualifying swing show nothing — no values are invented.

Demo only: Kalshi's demo exchange uses mock funds, and its prices may not reflect real markets. The primary data is the committed official demo-API capture (snapshot/charts.json, collected 2026-11-20T11:43:59Z from https://external-api.demo.kalshi.co/trade-api/v2). A news-search link is a starting point for investigation, not a verified cause of any swing.

Method: For each tracked market, consecutive candle closes are compared; a swing is recorded when |to − from| ≥ threshold. Each entry links to the market on Kalshi, a news search for that event (cause unverified), and the exact official candlestick request URL that produced the two closes. Default window is 1-hour candles over the trailing 7 days; the snapshot also carries 1-minute and 1-day closes, which are scanned too. Official candlesticks API ↗ · Demo environment warning ↗
High-ROI filter (disclosed heuristic, not a model): swings are scored by |Δ| ÷ from-price (the relative move on the entry price) and ranked highest first; only swings scoring ≥ 0.10 are shown, capped at 20. This is a transparent proxy for "high-ROI setups," not a trading model and not a recommendation. The five-topic filter stays gas prices · elections · culture · crypto · golf.
Not investment advice. Kalshi demo prices are mock-funds data. Unverified values are never filled in; the flagged items for this desk are tracked in Flagged for Review and COORDINATION.md.
