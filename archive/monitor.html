04 / TRADE ANALYSIS

What to measure before judging a strategy

A

Outcomes & costs

Group completed trades by topic, strategy tag, YES/NO side and entry price. Calculate realized returns only from actual fills, resolution payouts and fees.

B

Risk & execution

Compare intended versus filled price, slippage, spread, depth at requested size, concentration, and worst-case loss. Preserve timestamps and source records.

C

Evidence quality

Separate open positions from resolved outcomes. Report sample size, missing records and uncertainty; avoid claiming predictive edge from a small sample.
