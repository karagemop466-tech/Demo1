Orderbook — liquidity & contracts available at each price

Official convention: the book returns bids for both sides — a YES bid at p¢ is identical to a NO ask at (100−p)¢. Depth bars show quantity available at each price level. Values are normalized defensively; any field the API returns in an unexpected shape is flagged rather than mis-rendered.

YES side (bids, best first)

NO side (bids, best first)

How liquidity is computed: “Resting bids” = Σ quantity across all price levels on each side (from GET /markets/{ticker}/orderbook, field orderbook_fp). The market’s own liquidity field is shown as reported in the market details card. If the two disagree, both are shown — no reconciliation guess is made.
