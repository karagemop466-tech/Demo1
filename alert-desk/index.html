Method, sources & caveats (read before trading on this)

  • Threshold. A bubble requires an absolute move ≥ $0.10 between the two observed prices, in either direction (brief requirement). Nothing smaller is shown; nothing is inferred.
  • Historical bubbles come from committed engine/jumps.json: moves between official candle closes / book mids, computed by the engine desk from documented candlestick endpoint captures. Coverage is the markets the collector captured candles for (see coverage in the file) — uncovered markets are not evidence of no movement.
  • Live bubbles come from the browser polling the official public trades tape GET /trade-api/v2/markets/trades (demo base https://external-api.demo.kalshi.co/trade-api/v2). Each new trade is compared with the market's previously observed trade price; a ≥ $0.10 change emits a bubble. The tape is global, so live bubbles are not limited to candle-captured markets. If the browser cannot reach the API (CORS/egress), the page says so and shows the committed snapshot only.
  • Chat 2 filter (the "model"). Rule P: the market appears in the sim-desk automated ledger (positions_open/trades_settled). Rule B: side = YES for an up-jump / NO for a down-jump, entry = the post-jump price, probability = the resolved YES-frequency of that price band from the engine settlement study (2,469 resolved demo markets; band sample size n is shown on the badge), cost = entry + taker fee ceil(M×0.07×C×P×(1−P)) with M=1 (official fee schedule; settlement $1 per winning contract per official settlement page). ROI > 0 flags the bubble. Band frequencies are historical counts on demo data, not predictions.
  • Honest aggregate. The engine's fee-aware backtest over these strategies currently shows negative net ROI overall (−50.36% on its 16 simulated trades — source). "Model likes" is a transparent rule, not a claim of profit.
  • Jump ≠ cause. A news-search link opens a query; it is a lead only. No causal claim is made.
  • No credentials, no orders. This page only reads public data. Portfolio/order paths require signing and are intentionally absent here (see Flagged for Review).
  • Topic tags use this repo's published keyword rules (tools/collect_kalshi.py TOPIC_RULES) applied to title/ticker; a market matching no rule shows a blank topic with a reason — never guessed.
