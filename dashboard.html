Highest dollar-volume events on the demo exchange, restricted to the five requested topics, with a resolution tracker that keeps an event open until every leg prints yes or no.

How this ranking is built — and what it is not

Kalshi's API publishes contract counts (volume_fp), not dollars, so this table ranks by Σ (volume_fp × last_price_dollars) over each event's legs. That is an estimate: it prices a market's whole lifetime at its last trade rather than at each trade. Exact turnover needs GET /trades summed as price × count (S-1).

A floor badge means at least one leg had no usable volume field at capture, so the true figure is higher than shown. Estimated dollar figures are never presented as market dollars.

Reading the tracker

  • Legs settled n/m — the event stays “partial” until every leg has a result; the California ballot event is the worked example (3 of 4 settled, one leg still active).
  • Per-leg outcome badges come from the market's result field, which is only non-empty after determination — never inferred from price.
  • Payout/contract is settlement_value_dollars: $1.0000 for the winning side, $0.0000 for the losing side.
  • Still open lists the market tickers the poller must keep watching, which is exactly the set a resolution alert should subscribe to.
