How much rests at each price

The Kalshi order book publishes bids only, on both the YES and the NO side. The ask side is derived from the reciprocal relationship between the two. This page shows, for every price level, how many contracts are resting and what that depth is worth in dollars.

How the book is read
  • Level arrays are sorted ascending; the last element is the best (highest) bid.
  • A YES bid at $X is the same as a NO ask at $1.00 − X.
  • Depth shown is resting liquidity, not guaranteed fill size.
