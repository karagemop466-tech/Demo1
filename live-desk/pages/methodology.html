Data pipeline

  1. Fetch: request up to five pages of 100 open markets per refresh from the demo REST endpoint. Further pages can be loaded on demand.
  2. Classify: scan returned title, subtitle and tickers against visible keyword rules for gas, elections, culture, crypto and golf. This is provisional and not a completeness guarantee.
  3. Fetch book depth: request public orderbooks for the filtered market tickers in batches of at most 100. A request failure leaves book-derived fields blank and displays a warning.
  4. Group and rank: group returned markets by `event_ticker` where present (otherwise market ticker). The lead market title is displayed; book depth is aggregated across returned markets and book levels in the group. Ranking is only as complete as loaded market pages and returned books.
  5. Render: market prices and contract counts use the API fields. The depth amount is computed locally from dollars-per-contract × contracts. Rankings are not official Kalshi metrics.
  6. Track/chart: a starred market's ticker and observations are saved in the current browser. Chart midpoint is (reported YES bid + reported YES ask) / 2 only when both exist. Observation time is local collection time—not an exchange timestamp. The chart is not historical-price backfill.
  7. Resolution: saved ticker rechecks use the official single-market read endpoint; only returned `result` values `yes` or `no` are labeled resolved. Other outcomes/statuses are displayed from the response, not reinterpreted.

Reading the dashboard safely

  • Every “—” is unknown/not present in the response, not zero.
  • Volume and open interest are contract quantities. They are not shown with a dollar sign.
  • Visible bid depth is only resting YES/NO bid notional represented by returned book levels, for the set of markets loaded. It excludes anything not in the response and can change immediately.
  • A market-level result is not automatically proof that every contract in a related event resolved the same way; inspect the market ticker and official source response.
  • Keyword category tags and browser-derived values are project output, not a Kalshi endorsement or official classification.
  • Potential browser CORS behavior is unverified; dashboard errors remain explicit instead of replacing data with examples.
