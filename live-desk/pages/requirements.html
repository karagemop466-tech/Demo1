Improvement suggestions — proposed, not implemented

1 · Make a validated topic catalog

Replace keyword-only matching with an approved Kalshi event/series allowlist and explicit inclusion/exclusion reasons; label missing catalog coverage.

High valueEvidence needed: candidate series and event list for each requested topic.

2 · Adopt a transparent liquidity definition

Show event-level observed bid depth separately from contract volume, open interest, best bid/ask, and timestamp. Display book coverage and price-level details so one measure is not mistaken for all liquidity.

High valueEvidence needed: approved definition, grouping and coverage boundary.

3 · Keep a durable resolution and data audit trail

Persist timestamped market snapshots and status/result transitions in a backend, preserving ticker, source URL, fetch time and payload fields. The current local browser history is not a reliable shared archive.

High valueEvidence needed: retention, storage and access policy.

4 · Secure portfolio integration separately from Pages

Use a server-side signing component with the minimum required API scopes, secrets management, audit logs and a demo-only environment boundary. Do not store private keys in JavaScript or local storage.

Critical safetyEvidence needed: account owner authorization and deployment owner.

5 · Define strategy evaluation before enabling trades

Agree on trade tags, realized/unrealized P&L, fees, exposure, hold time, drawdown and sample-size disclosures before comparing strategies. Add caps, idempotency, preflight, confirmations and a kill switch only after explicit approval.

Critical safetyEvidence needed: operational risk rules and validated demo test plan.
