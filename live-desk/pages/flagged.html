Non-blocking implementation notes

The first dashboard release is deliberately limited to public demo read endpoints, visible-book calculations, client-side observations, and explicit missing-data states. These limits should stay visible until replaced by verified alternatives. Report a new candidate issue by adding a row here with evidence and an official source link.

No external market values, candidate events, account balances, trade records or outcomes have been inserted as examples. Live rows are only produced by a successful source response.
