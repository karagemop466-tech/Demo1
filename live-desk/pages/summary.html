Current application behavior

  • Reads open market pages and multi-market orderbooks from the recommended demo REST host, and filters a loaded sample to the five requested themes.
  • Ranks event groups by computed visible bid notional by default; alternative rankings show Kalshi-reported 24-hour volume or open interest in contracts, not USD.
  • Displays reported bid/ask and contract metrics only when the API returns fields; unavailable values stay blank.
  • Collects price-chart observations only while this browser has the dashboard open; the chart does not backfill historical exchange prices.
  • Stores a small watchlist and chart history in the current browser. It rechecks saved tickers for closure/resolution when public API access succeeds.
  • Does not perform trading, authenticate the account, or calculate portfolio performance until a separate secure service and account authorization are designed and reviewed.
Decision-safe default: keep this interface read-only. A production-grade order workflow should be a separately secured demo-only service with explicit pre-trade checks, caps, audit logging and an operator confirmation step.

Critical decisions still needed

These are unknown—not values to assume. The full question register and discovery actions are in Requirements & decisions.

  • What exact listing families qualify for each topic, and what does “highest dollar amount” mean: visible book depth, traded notional, or open interest valued at prices?
  • What secure backend/authentication deployment is approved for the demo account, and what are the account/API permissions?
  • What trading guardrails, event eligibility rules, strategy labels, attribution window and performance definitions are required?
  • What is the desired historical chart retention and collection frequency, and where may those observations be stored?
