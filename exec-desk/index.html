Kalshi Demo Dashboard

A clean, live dashboard over the Kalshi demo Trade API — events tracked to resolution, portfolio & trade analysis, price/liquidity charts, reviewable suggestions, and a fully sourced verification trail. Data is fetched live in your browser from the official demo endpoints; nothing on this site is pre-filled or guessed.

Executive summary

What was built, requirement-by-requirement delivery table, key official facts, top risks and the first decisions to make.

Live dashboard

Top dollar-amount live events across five topics · price chart with seconds/minutes/hours x-axis · liquidity depth with contracts available at each price.

Markets & resolution

Polls open markets until the exchange determines them; records how each resolved (YES/NO) with timestamps; settled history per topic.

Portfolio

Official demo-account balance/positions/settlements (blank until a key is added) plus the paper ledger: P&L, win rate, ROI by category and strategy.

Execute & analyze

Trade ticket against live quotes — SIMULATED paper fills or REAL demo orders; auto-categorization; strategy tags; open positions with live marks.

Suggestions

Dedicated review section: curated + live auto-suggestions, Accept/Reject/Defer, and an implementation path picker — nothing changes without a decision.

Questions & answers

What we know (sourced), what we don't (missing information named), and what must be decided before moving — with discovery steps.

Flagged for review
