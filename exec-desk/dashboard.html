How to read this page

Live, not stored

Every number is fetched from external-api.demo.kalshi.co when this page loads and re-polled while open. Nothing on this page is pre-filled.

Seconds vs minutes vs hours

Official candlesticks exist only at 1 / 60 / 1440-minute intervals. “Seconds” mode therefore samples the official quote every few seconds in your browser (UNVERIFIED-07).

Resolution

Markets run until they resolve YES or NO; settlement pays $1 per winning contract (docs). Track full resolution history on the Markets page.
