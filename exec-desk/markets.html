Resolution rules (official)

• On a Yes outcome, each Yes contract pays $1; on a No outcome, each No contract pays $1 (source: Market Settlement).
• Market lifecycle: initialized → inactive → active → closed → determined → (disputed) → finalized (source: Get Markets).
• result is yes, no, scalar or empty while pending.
• Settlement typically follows expiration shortly; timing varies by data source and review (source: Market Settlement docs).

How this page tracks resolutions

1. Fetch open events for the topic's highest-volume series (GET /events?status=open&with_nested_markets=true).
2. Re-poll every 60s; when a market's status becomes determined/finalized and result is non-empty, write an entry to the Resolution watch log (browser-local) with ticker, result and observation time.
3. The Resolved view pulls GET /events?status=settled for the same series so past resolutions stay visible.
