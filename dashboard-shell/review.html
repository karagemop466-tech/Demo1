FindingEvidence, impact and statusOfficial source
U-01 · CoverageThe overview watches the finite committed watchlist and reads at most three 1,000-trade pages per poll. If the cap or an error is reached, the page reports partial data. Polling, ordering ambiguities and the reused engine's five-minute deduplication mean some price changes can be missed. A blank feed is not proof that no market moved. Engine implementation ↗Get Trades and pagination ↗
U-02 · RankingThe newer event collection is bounded and ranks a 24-hour volume × last-price proxy, not actual traded dollars. Its collector substitutes zero for missing inputs in event totals and truncates some saved market lists, so the chosen candidates are not a verified global ranking. The overview blanks and flags estimates it cannot recompute from complete saved inputs. The separate older collection contains tape-calculated notional for only a subset of contracts. Live prices do not make either historical ranking current.Event / market fields ↗ · Trade fields ↗
U-03 · Topic assignmentTopic membership follows published rules and may miss or misclassify a market; other repository dashboards use different classifiers. Prior review notes that an older elections page included the KXWTI oil series. Membership is not an official Kalshi certification. Earlier findings ↗Series metadata ↗
U-04 · FreshnessWatchlist, fallback chart and example alerts are dated snapshots, not live results. A successful API request does not mean every watched market has just refreshed. The page shows the time associated with each market, book and chart. Browser connectivity, CORS and background throttling vary.Public market data ↗
U-05 · ModelThe model is a simple gross winning-case screen, not an estimate of expected ROI or win probability. It does not account for fees, order size, slippage or fill certainty. Live screening requires a quote observed within 90 seconds; snapshot examples remain historical.Settlement ↗ · Order book ↗

Their order-book links identify the individual tickers, but the market-list entry source does not match either position's series. This is a provenance defect, not proof that the simulated fill or resulting P&L is correct or incorrect.
