PROJECT BRIEF

Executive summary.

A focused workspace for observing Kalshi demo markets in gas prices, elections, culture, crypto and golf. It combines an automatic public-data watchlist with two alert feeds, an inspectable chart and order book, and links to paper-trading results. It does not place orders or connect to an account.

Demo is not production. Kalshi describes this environment as using mock funds and cautions that demo prices and behavior may not reflect real markets. An API-reachable badge is not a promise of complete market or alert coverage. Official demo guidance ↗

How to interpret the ranking

The leading-event table selects the first candidate in each topic from a regularly collected, bounded event snapshot. It ranks using a proxy: the sum of volume_24h_fp × last_price_dollars over an event's contracts. The proxy is not actual dollar volume, and it cannot establish the globally highest-dollar live events. The source collector treats missing inputs as zero when choosing ranks; its saved event list can also be truncated. The dashboard therefore displays the estimated dollar amount only when every saved market input is present, the list is untruncated and the calculation reconciles; otherwise it is blank and flagged. The selection itself remains subject to those limits. Official event and market fields ↗

The separate older watchlist shows “24h YES notional”: the repository collector's sum of yes_price_dollars × count_fp over captured public trades for a contract, when that collector marked coverage complete. It is not an official dollar-volume field, total event notional or current liquidity. Missing values remain blank. Live prices may update while both ranking datasets remain historical.
