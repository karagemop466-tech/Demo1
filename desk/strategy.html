Strategies & paper trades

Eight rules, written down before any trade, run automatically against the official demo snapshot. Each fill pays taker fees from the published schedule and is graded against the exchange's own result once the market settles. This is a simulation for comparing trade types — it is not a track record, and no order left this repository.

Why so few graded trades. The demo snapshot was taken once, and a trade can only be graded when a later observation shows the market finalised. Long-dated markets (2028 nominees, end-of-year crypto ranges) will not resolve for months. The runner adds samples every 30 minutes and grades whatever has settled, so these numbers grow over time without any manual step.

The rules, as published before the run

IDStrategyFamilyEntry ruleWhy it is interesting
S1Topic favourite (momentum)DirectionalInside each topic, buy YES on the market with the highest 24h traded notional when its YES ask is between $0.02 and $0.98.Tests whether the money in the demo book is on the eventual winner.
S2Topic underdog (contrarian)DirectionalInside each topic, buy YES on the lowest-priced market whose YES ask is >= $0.02 and whose 24h traded notional is >= $100.Long-shot bias test: cheap YES contracts often overprice tails.
S3YES/NO pair arbitrageRelative valueBuy 1 YES and 1 NO in the same market when YES ask + NO ask < $1.00 by at least $0.01 after taker fees on both legs.A $1.00 guaranteed pair bought below $1.00 is a riskless edge if both legs fill.
S4Heavy favourite (hold YES)Relative valueBuy YES where the YES ask is between $0.80 and $0.95 and the implied spread (YES ask + NO ask - $1.00) is at most $0.02, i.e. the book agrees with the favourite and the round trip is cheap.Favourite-longshot bias: heavily favoured contracts historically win slightly more often than their price implies. Tested here on demo prices.
S5Wide-spread fadeLiquidityBuy NO where the implied spread (YES ask + NO ask - $1.00) >= $0.05 and the NO ask is between $0.02 and $0.98, i.e. the book is wide enough to justify taking the safer side.Wide books on the demo venue mean the quoted price is not a fair estimate.
S6Balanced book (deepest contracts)LiquidityBuy YES on the market with the largest resting YES-ask contract count among markets whose YES ask is at most $0.60.Checks whether liquidity, not opinion, predicts the outcome.
S7Crypto range midpointStructuralInside crypto only, buy YES on the market whose YES mid is closest to $0.50 with a spread <= $0.10.Crypto range markets cluster; the near-coin-flip contract is the purest test.
S8Short-dated holderEvent outcomeBuy YES on the market that closes soonest (within 7 days of the snapshot) where the YES price is between $0.20 and $0.80.Fast settlement gives the most graded samples per collection run.
