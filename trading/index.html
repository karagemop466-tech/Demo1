Automated demo strategy

spread-capture-lite

Implemented in scripts/run-demo-trade.mjs. For each candidate market it reads the public order book and only acts when the spread is wide enough:

  • Require the implied YES ask (1 − best NO bid) to exceed the best YES bid by at least the configured edge.
  • Place a single passive bid for the YES leg at the best YES bid — it never crosses the spread.
  • Respect a price band and a maximum contracts-per-order cap.
  • Stop after the configured number of orders per run.

The runner refuses to place anything unless KALSHI_ENABLE_TRADING=1 is set and a demo key is present, and it writes every attempt — accepted, rejected or skipped — to the journal below.

Order-side semantics

The V2 endpoint quotes everything from the YES leg: bid means buy YES and ask means sell YES. Selling YES is economically the same as buying NO at 1 − price. The ticket shows both numbers so the direction is never ambiguous.
