Demo venue. Kalshi's own docs say the demo environment runs on mock funds and that "the price and behavior of markets in the demo environment may not be reflective of those in real markets" (demo_env). Nothing here is a real-money book, and nothing here placed an order: there is no demo API key in this repository, so the trade and portfolio numbers are SIMULATED and labelled as such. Blank cells mean the API did not return a value — they are never filled in.

Official API response
Y = contract price in dollars ($0.00–$1.00) · X = time, minutes.
Contracts available at each price YES bids are resting orders on the YES side. A YES ask is not returned by the API: it is the documented identity 1.00 − highest NO bid (orderbook_responses).

A jump is two adjacent candles in the official candlestick response whose recorded close differs by at least the threshold. The move is a fact about the tape; the cause is not. The news link is a search over the event title, not a source — nothing here asserts why a price moved, and on thin demo books a single stray lot can print a "jump". Filter to 1-minute candles to see only moves that mattered inside a minute.

Every market is tracked until the exchange sets an outcome. On a settled market result is yes, no or (for scalar markets) scalar, and settlement_value_dollars / settlement_ts carry the payout — statuses and their meaning are in the official market lifecycle page. A market past its close time with no result yet is still awaiting determination, so the result cell stays blank and is marked as such rather than guessed.

Three rules replayed over captured candles, plus a study of what holding to settlement would have paid at every price bucket. Costs are real: each leg crosses the recorded spread and pays the published taker fee round up(M × 0.07 × C × P × (1 − P)) (fee schedule, effective July 7, 2026; no settlement or membership fee). These are simulated fills on demo data, not account history — and they are a small sample, so treat every number as a measurement of this tape, not an edge.

How this page gets its numbers. Tables and charts render from strategies.json / jumps.json — files computed by tools/strategy_sim.py and tools/jump_scan.py from a capture of the official demo API, each field stamped with the request URL that returned it. When the browser can reach the demo API (public, unauthenticated; CORS evidenced in ../data/cors.json) the selected market is polled live and the badge goes green. No manual input, no build step, no credentials, no order ever placed.
