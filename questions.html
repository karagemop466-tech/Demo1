QuestionAnswerSource
Are the endpoints in the brief the right ones?Yes — both the recommended and the shared demo hosts match Kalshi's own table, REST and WebSocket.demo env
Is the demo exchange actually up?Yes at capture: exchange_active and trading_active both true across four shards./exchange/status
Do market-data reads need a key?No. Only portfolio and order operations do; public reads are unauthenticated but rate-limited separately.market data · limits
Is demo money real money?No — “a demo environment with mock funds”, and its prices “may not be reflective of those in real markets”.demo env
Can we rank by dollar amount straight from the API?No. Markets expose volume_fp in contracts; dollars must be computed (proxy) or summed from trades (exact).Get Markets
Can the graph show seconds?Not from REST: candlesticks are 1-minute, 1-hour, 1-day only. Seconds require the authenticated WS feed or trade timestamps.Get Market Candlesticks
How do we know an event resolved, and how?Per market: result = “yes”/“no”, confirmed by settlement_value_dollars 1.0000/0.0000 and settlement_ts. An event is done when no leg is pending.settled rows
What do trades cost?Taker 0.07·C·P·(1−P) rounded up, maker 0.0175·…, no settlement fee; the gas series runs multipliers 1/1.fee schedule
What is the correct order endpoint today?POST /portfolio/events/orders with the V2 body; legacy /portfolio/orders writes are gone (410).quick start · Create Order (V2)

What is not a question

To keep the register honest, these were the tempting items that turned out to be answered by the data or the docs, so they are not listed above: whether the demo host works, whether market data is free to read, whether resolution is observable, what fees are, what the order endpoint is, whether seconds come from REST, whether Elections and Politics are distinct categories, whether /series paginates, and whether an empty book means a dead market.

Suggested discovery order

  1. Q-3 first — it is free, takes one click, and decides the architecture.
  2. Q-1 + Q-2 together — the only credential decision; everything account-shaped follows it.
  3. Q-4, Q-5, Q-10 — three product definitions, five minutes of reading.
  4. Q-6, Q-7 — topic scoping; until then the dashboard ranks what exists rather than what you meant.
  5. Q-11 before any order, always.
