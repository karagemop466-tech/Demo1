Trade categorization

Proposed tags

  • Topic: site-side category / keyword match (gas prices, elections, culture, crypto, golf).
  • Direction: the API's reported YES/NO side and buy/sell action.
  • Execution: the API's reported taker flag, book side, contract count, price, and fee fields.
  • Lifecycle: open, closed, and the market's explicit YES/NO result. A market-level result is not silently generalized to an entire multi-market event.

These tags are analysis dimensions only. They do not assert that a trade followed a proven or profitable strategy.
